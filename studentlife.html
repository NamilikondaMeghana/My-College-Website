StudentLife of Pragnya Degree College

Student life at Pragnya Degree College is a combination of academic learning,
personal development and extracurricular activities.
The college provides students with an opportunity to learn new
things, develop their skills and build friendships. Students
participate in classroom activities, seminars, presentations,
cultural programmes and various college events.

Flexibility

  • Courses are designed by the college itself, not strictly by a university.
  • Students can learn modern technologies and updated subjects.
  • Internal assessments, projects and practical learning are encouraged.

2. Skill Development

  • Regular workshops, hackathons and coding contests are conducted.
  • Communication and leadership skills are developed through seminars and events.
  • Students get exposure to real-world problems and innovation challenges.

3. Festivals and Events

  • Tech fests, Cultural Fests and Sports Meets are organized by students.
  • Students plan, manage and participate in different activities.
  • These events improve creativity, teamwork and confidence.

4. Internships and Industry Exposure

  • Students get opportunities to participate in internships.
  • Placement training helps students prepare for job interviews.
  • Industry interactions provide practical knowledge and career guidance.
