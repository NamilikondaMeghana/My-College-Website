BCA(Bachelor of Computer Applications)

Pragnya Degree College offers a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
course for students who are interested in computers,
programming and information technology. The BCA course is a three-year
undergraduate degree programme that focuses on developing
computer knowledge, programming skills Students learn subjects such as
C, C++, Java, Python, Database Management Systems, Web Development.

Mathematics

Mathematics at Pragnya Degree College plays an important role in
developing students' logical thinking, problem-solving abilities and
analytical skills. The subject helps students understand mathematical
concepts and apply them in real-life situations. Students learn
topics such as algebra, calculus, statistics, probability and other
important mathematical concepts depending on their course and
syllabus.

HOD:Durga Prasanna

BCOM

B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce) at Pragnya Degree College is an undergraduate
programme designed for students who are interested in commerce, business,
finance and accounting. The course generally has a duration of three years
and helps students develop knowledge of business
management, financial accounting, economics, taxation, banking and
business laws. Students gain an understanding of financial activities,
business operations and management principles.

HOD:Mamatha
